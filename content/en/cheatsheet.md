title: English I — Cheat Sheet

## 1. Narrative tenses (week 2)

| Tense | Form | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Past simple | V2 / did + V1 | main events in order |
| Past continuous | was/were + V-ing | background action in progress |
| Past perfect | had + V3 | an earlier past event |
| Past perfect continuous | had been + V-ing | a long action up to an earlier past moment (*for / since*) |

## 2. Conversation (weeks 3, 8, 12)

| Agree | Partly agree | Disagree politely |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I couldn't agree more. · Exactly. | I agree up to a point, but… · That's true, but… | I'm not sure about that. · I see it differently. |

**Opinions:** In my opinion… · As far as I'm concerned… · If you ask me… · I'm in two minds. · I'm not convinced.
**Discourse markers:** *by the way* (new side topic) · *anyway* (back to main point) · *actually* (= in fact, correction — not "now") · *as I was saying* (after an interruption) · *speaking of…* · *mind you* (limits the last point) · *to be honest*.

## 3. Reading and listening (week 4)

**Gist** = main idea (skim: title, first/last sentences). **Detail** = scan for key words, numbers, names. Questions use **synonyms**. **False** = text says the opposite; **Not given** = text says nothing.

## 4. Senses and articles (week 5)

*look / sound / feel / smell / taste* + **adjective** (*sounds great*) · + **like + noun** (*sounds like a plan*).
**a** (consonant sound: *a university*) · **an** (vowel sound: *an hour*) · **the** (specific, second mention, only one) · **Ø** (general plurals/uncountables: *Ø Cats are…*).

## 5. How-to web article (week 6)

Catchy title (*How to… / 5 ways to…*) → short intro → numbered tips with **imperatives** → conclusion. **must** = necessary · **mustn't** = forbidden · **don't have to** = not necessary · **should** = advice.

## 6. Future forms (week 8)

**will** (prediction, quick decision) · **going to** (plan, evidence now) · **present continuous** (arrangement) · **will be + V-ing** (in progress at a future time) · **will have + V3** (finished *by* a future time). After *when/if/as soon as* use the **present**.

## 7. Relative clauses with prepositions (week 9)

Formal: *the city **in which** she lives* · *the man **with whom** I work* · *ten students, **most of whom**…* Never *in that* / *with who*. *in which* = *where* (place), *when* (time).

## 8. Passive (week 10)

**be + V3**: is made · was invented · has been banned · is being tested · will be recycled · can be reused. No passive for verbs without an object (*happen, arrive*). Talk signposts: *Today I'd like to talk about… · First of all… · Let's move on to… · To sum up…*

## 9. Compound nouns and possessives (week 11)

*search engine, password, e-book* — first noun **singular** (*a shoe shop, a ten-minute video*). **'s** for people/time (*my sister's laptop, today's news*); **s'** for regular plurals (*the students' bags*); *children's, people's*. Paragraph: topic sentence → 2 advantages → *On the other hand* 2 disadvantages → *On balance…*

## 10. Past modals of deduction (week 13)

**must have + V3** (almost sure it happened) · **might / may / could have + V3** (possible) · **can't / couldn't have + V3** (almost sure it didn't). Never *mustn't have* for deduction; never *must of*.

## 11. Substitution (week 14)

**one / ones** (countable nouns) · **it / they / this** (things / ideas) · **so / not** after *think, hope, be afraid* (*I think so / I hope not*) · **do / did / do so** (verb phrases) · **the same**, **such**.

## 12. Comparison paragraph (week 15)

Similar: *both…and · like · similarly · likewise · as…as*. Different: *whereas · while · unlike · however · in contrast · much/far more…than · not as…as*. Organise **point by point**: each point compares A and B.
