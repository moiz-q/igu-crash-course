In exams you often meet a long text and only a few minutes. Good readers do **not** read every word slowly the first time. They use two different kinds of reading (or listening):

## Gist vs. detail

| | **Reading for gist** | **Reading for detail** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question you ask | *What is this text mainly about?* | *Exactly what / when / how many / why?* |
| Speed | fast, one quick read | slow, careful, only the part you need |
| What you look at | title, first and last sentences of each paragraph, repeated words | key words from the question, numbers, names |
| Typical exam task | "Choose the best title", "What is the main idea?" | "According to the text, how many…?", "True / False / Not given" |

### A simple method (works for listening too)

1. **Before:** read the questions first and underline key words.
2. **First read / listen — gist:** do not stop for unknown words. Ask: *topic? writer's opinion?*
3. **Second read / listen — detail:** find the part of the text that matches each question. Look for **synonyms**: the question often uses different words from the text (*"pets"* in the question, *"companion animals"* in the text).
4. **Check:** is the answer really stated, or are you guessing? If it is not in the text, it is **Not given**.

## Vocabulary: talking about animals and attitudes

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **wildlife** | animals living free in nature |
| **endangered** | in danger of disappearing completely |
| **habitat** | the natural home of an animal |
| **domestic / pet** | living with people |
| **stray** (cat, dog) | without a home, living on the streets |
| **cruelty** | causing pain on purpose |
| **animal welfare** | the health and good treatment of animals |
| **to protect / to conserve** | to keep safe |
| **to be fond of** | to like a lot |
| **to treat (well / badly)** | to behave towards |

## Practice: vocabulary gap-fill (15 items)

_ Pandas are ___; there are fewer than 2,000 in the wild. = endangered
_ Cutting down forests destroys the ___ of many birds. = habitat | habitats
_ Istanbul is famous for its ___ cats that live in the streets. = stray
_ My grandmother is very ___ of her old dog. = fond
_ Hitting an animal for fun is an act of ___. = cruelty
_ The charity works for animal ___: food, shelter and vet care. = welfare
_ National parks ___ wildlife from hunters. = protect | conserve
_ Cows and sheep are ___ animals; wolves are wild. = domestic | farm
_ We saw a lot of ___ on our trip: deer, eagles and foxes. = wildlife
_ Some people ___ their pets like members of the family. = treat
_ Reading quickly for the main idea is called reading for ___. = gist
_ Reading carefully for numbers and names is reading for ___. = detail | details
_ If the answer is not in the text, the answer is "not ___". = given
_ "Companion animals" is a ___ for "pets". = synonym
_ Before you listen, read the ___ first. = questions | question

## Reading: Istanbul's street cats (≈300 words)

First read in **60 seconds** for the gist. Then answer the questions, reading again for detail.

> Visitors to Istanbul are often surprised by the number of cats. They sleep on café chairs, sit in shop windows and wait politely outside butchers. Nobody knows the exact number, but some estimates suggest there are more than a hundred thousand street cats in the city.
>
> Unlike in many other countries, these animals are not usually seen as a problem. Most belong to nobody and to everybody at the same time. Shopkeepers leave out bowls of water, neighbours bring leftover food, and some local councils build small wooden houses to keep the cats warm in winter. In several districts, volunteers and vets work together to vaccinate and neuter the animals so that the population stays healthy and does not grow too fast.
>
> This attitude has a long history. Cats were welcome in Ottoman cities because they kept mice away from food stores and libraries. Many people also say that caring for animals is simply part of being a good neighbour. A popular documentary about the cats, released in 2016, made them famous around the world.
>
> However, not everyone is completely happy. Some residents complain about noise at night or about food left on the pavements. Bird lovers point out that cats hunt small birds. Animal welfare groups, on the other hand, worry most about cats that are injured by traffic or that do not get enough food during long, cold winters.
>
> Experts agree that the best approach is a balance: feeding stations in fixed places, regular vet programmes and education in schools. In this way, the city can keep one of its most loved traditions while reducing the problems.

? **Gist:** Which is the best title for the text?
- How to Adopt a Cat in Turkey ~~ adoption is not discussed
* Istanbul's Shared Street Cats: Tradition and Challenges ~~ covers care, history, problems and solutions
- Why Cats Are Dangerous for Birds ~~ only one small detail
- A Famous 2016 Documentary ~~ mentioned only once
! A good title covers the whole text, not one paragraph.

? **Detail:** According to the text, why were cats welcome in Ottoman cities?
- They were expensive pets ~~ not stated
* They kept mice away from food and libraries ~~ paragraph 3
- They were used in films ~~ the film is from 2016
- The government paid people to keep them ~~ not stated
! Find the paragraph about history (paragraph 3).

? **Detail:** What do volunteers and vets do together?
- Build roads ~~ not stated
- Take cats to other countries ~~ not stated
* Vaccinate and neuter the cats ~~ paragraph 2
- Close cafés ~~ not stated
! Look for the key words "volunteers" and "vets".

? **True, False or Not given:** "Most Istanbul residents want the cats removed from the streets."
- True ~~ the text says the cats are usually not seen as a problem
* False ~~ the text says most people do not see them as a problem; only some residents complain
- Not given ~~ the text does talk about people's attitude
! "Some residents complain" is not the same as "most residents".

? What does the writer think is the best solution?
- Removing all the cats ~~ not suggested
* A balance of feeding stations, vet programmes and education ~~ last paragraph
- Stopping all feeding ~~ not suggested
- Only education in schools ~~ only one part of the solution
! The writer's view is often in the last paragraph.

## Listening-style practice (transcript)

In the exam you may hear a short talk. Here we give the **transcript**: cover it, ask a friend to read it aloud, or read it once quickly (gist) and then slowly (detail).

> *"Good morning, everyone. Today I want to talk about zoos. Fifty years ago, most zoos kept animals in small cages, mainly so that visitors could look at them. Today the best zoos have three aims: conservation, education and research. For example, the snow leopard breeding programme in Europe now includes more than 200 animals. Critics still argue that large animals such as elephants should never live in zoos, because they need to walk many kilometres a day. So the question is not only 'Are zoos good or bad?' but 'Which animals can live well in a zoo?'"*

:::reveal Check your answers
- **Gist:** how zoos have changed and the debate about which animals should live in them.
- **Detail 1:** the three aims of modern zoos are *conservation, education and research*.
- **Detail 2:** the snow leopard programme includes *more than 200* animals.
- **Detail 3:** critics say elephants should not live in zoos because they *need to walk many kilometres a day*.
:::

## Try it

Write **80–100 words**: *"How do people in your town treat animals? Give one good example and one problem."* Use at least **four** words from the vocabulary table.

:::reveal Show model answer
In my town, most people are fond of animals, especially the stray cats and dogs that live near the market. Shopkeepers often leave out water, and a local charity runs a small shelter where vets vaccinate homeless animals. This is a good example of animal welfare that comes from neighbours, not only from the government. However, there is also a problem. Some drivers go too fast in the narrow streets, and every month a few animals are injured. I believe the council should build more speed bumps to protect both animals and children.
:::

:::rubric Writing self-check
- Clear topic sentence about attitudes in your town
- One good example **and** one problem
- At least four vocabulary items used correctly
- 80–100 words, short clear sentences
:::

## Common mistakes

- Reading every word slowly the first time and running out of time.
- Choosing an answer because it contains the **same word** as the text. Exam questions often use synonyms — check the meaning.
- Mixing **False** (the text says the opposite) and **Not given** (the text says nothing about it).
- *"The cats are very fond to people"* → *fond **of***.

## Key terms

- gist | the general meaning of a text | ana fikir, genel anlam
- skimming | reading quickly to get the gist | göz gezdirme
- scanning | searching a text quickly for one specific piece of information | tarama (belirli bilgi arama)
- endangered | in danger of disappearing completely | nesli tükenmekte olan
- animal welfare | the good treatment and health of animals | hayvan refahı

## Quick check

? You need to find **the year** a law was passed in a long article. You should:
- read every word from the beginning ~~ too slow
* scan the text for numbers that look like years ~~ scanning finds one specific detail fast
- read only the title ~~ the year is unlikely to be in the title
- guess ~~ you can find it in the text
! Scanning = looking quickly for a specific detail.

? The text says "Some residents complain about noise." The statement is "All residents love the cats." The answer is:
- True ~~ some residents complain
* False ~~ the text contradicts "all residents love"
- Not given ~~ the text gives information about residents' feelings
- Both true and false ~~ choose one
! False = the text says the opposite.

? Which word means "the natural home of an animal"?
- shelter ~~ a place people build for homeless animals
* habitat ~~ correct
- welfare ~~ good treatment
- wildlife ~~ the animals themselves
! *Habitat* = natural home.
