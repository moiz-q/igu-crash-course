# Atatürk Principles and History of Revolutions I — 25-question bank

? [01] Which Ottoman sultan abolished the Janissary corps in 1826 as part of military modernization?
- Selim III ~~ Selim III created the Nizam-ı Cedid army earlier, before being deposed in 1807.
* Mahmud II ~~ Mahmud II abolished the Janissaries in 1826 (the Auspicious Incident / *Vaka-i Hayriye*).
- Abdülmecid I ~~ Abdülmecid I issued the Tanzimat Edict in 1839.
- Abdülhamid II ~~ Abdülhamid II reigned from 1876 to 1909.
! Mahmud II abolished the Janissaries in 1826 and modernized Ottoman ministries and schools.

? [01] What did the Tanzimat Edict (1839) primarily promise?
- Immediate transition to a republic ~~ The Republic was proclaimed in 1923, not 1839.
* Equal protection of life, honour and property for all subjects, plus fairer taxes and conscription ~~ Read at Gülhane on 3 Nov 1839, it laid the foundation for legal equality.
- Complete abolition of the Sultanate ~~ The Sultanate was abolished in 1922.
- Entry into World War I ~~ WWI began in 1914.
! The 1839 Tanzimat Edict promised security of life, honour and property for all Ottoman subjects regardless of religion.

? [01] When was the First Constitutional Era (*I. Meşrutiyet*) proclaimed?
- 1839 ~~ 1839 was the Tanzimat Edict.
- 1856 ~~ 1856 was the Reform Edict (*Islahat Fermanı*).
* 1876 ~~ The 1876 Constitution (*Kanun-ı Esasi*) opened the first Ottoman parliament.
- 1908 ~~ 1908 was the Second Constitutional Era.
! The First Constitutional Era began in 1876 with the proclamation of the *Kanun-ı Esasi*.

? [01] Which intellectual movement argued that all Ottoman subjects, regardless of religion or ethnicity, should share equal citizenship to keep the empire united?
* Ottomanism (*Osmanlıcılık*) ~~ Ottomanism aimed to unite all subjects under a shared Ottoman civic identity.
- Islamism (*İslamcılık*) ~~ Islamism focused on uniting Muslim populations around the Caliphate.
- Turkism (*Türkçülük*) ~~ Turkism emphasized Turkish national identity and language.
- Westernism (*Batıcılık*) ~~ Westernism emphasized adopting European science, technology and institutions.
! Ottomanism (*Osmanlıcılık*) promoted equal Ottoman citizenship across all communities.

? [01] Which war in 1911–1912 pitted the Ottoman Empire against Italy in North Africa?
- Crimean War ~~ The Crimean War took place in 1853–1856.
* Tripoli War (*Trablusgarp Savaşı*) ~~ Italy invaded Ottoman Tripolitania/Cyrenaica (Libya) in 1911.
- First Balkan War ~~ The Balkan Wars were fought in 1912–1913 in Southeastern Europe.
- Russo-Turkish War ~~ The 1877–78 war took place in the Balkans and Caucasus.
! In the Tripoli War (1911–1912), Mustafa Kemal and other officers organized local resistance in Libya.

? [01] What was the major territorial consequence of the Balkan Wars (1912–1913) for the Ottoman Empire?
- Gain of new provinces in Central Europe ~~ The empire lost territory rather than gaining it.
* Loss of almost all European territories except Eastern Thrace ~~ Only Eastern Thrace (with Edirne recovered in 1913) remained in Europe.
- Loss of Anatolia ~~ Anatolia remained Ottoman until WWI.
- Partition of Egypt ~~ Egypt had already come under British control in 1882.
! The Balkan Wars cost the Ottoman Empire nearly all of its remaining European lands.

? [01] On which World War I front did Mustafa Kemal first gain widespread fame in 1915?
* Gallipoli (*Çanakkale*) ~~ His leadership at Arıburnu and Anafartalar helped halt the Allied landings.
- Tripoli ~~ Tripoli was the 1911–12 war against Italy.
- Suez Canal ~~ Mustafa Kemal did not command the 1915 Suez expedition.
- Galicia ~~ Galicia was an Eastern European front where other Ottoman units served.
! Mustafa Kemal's decisive actions at Gallipoli (1915) made him a national hero.

? [01] Which article of the Armistice of Mudros (30 Oct 1918) gave the Allies the right to occupy any strategic point if they claimed their security was threatened?
- Article 1 ~~ Article 1 dealt with opening the Straits.
* Article 7 ~~ Article 7 was the key clause used to justify Allied occupations across Anatolia.
- Article 15 ~~ Article 15 dealt with railways and telegraph lines.
- Article 20 ~~ Article 20 covered demobilization details.
! Article 7 of the Armistice of Mudros became the legal pretext for widespread Allied occupations.

? [01] On what date did Greek forces occupy İzmir, triggering mass protests and local armed resistance?
- 30 October 1918 ~~ That is the date of the Armistice of Mudros.
* 15 May 1919 ~~ Greek troops landed in İzmir on 15 May 1919, four days before Samsun.
- 19 May 1919 ~~ On 19 May 1919 Mustafa Kemal landed at Samsun.
- 23 April 1920 ~~ On 23 April 1920 the TBMM opened in Ankara.
! The occupation of İzmir on 15 May 1919 galvanized the *Kuvâ-yi Milliye* resistance.

? [01] Which event on 19 May 1919 is regarded as the beginning of the Turkish War of Independence?
- Signing of the Treaty of Lausanne ~~ Signed on 24 July 1923.
- Opening of the TBMM ~~ Opened on 23 April 1920.
* Mustafa Kemal's landing at Samsun ~~ He arrived in Samsun as 9th Army Inspector to organize national resistance.
- Battle of Sakarya ~~ Fought in August–September 1921.
! Mustafa Kemal landed at Samsun on 19 May 1919 (commemorated as Youth and Sports Day).

? [01] What key principle was first announced in the Amasya Circular (22 June 1919)?
- Acceptance of an American mandate ~~ Mandates were firmly rejected at Erzurum and Sivas.
* The nation's independence will be saved by the nation's own will and resolve ~~ It shifted the struggle from the Sultan's government to popular sovereignty.
- Immediate abolition of the Caliphate ~~ The Caliphate was abolished in 1924.
- Moving the capital to İzmir ~~ Ankara became the centre of the movement and later the capital.
! The Amasya Circular (22 June 1919) declared that the nation's own will and resolve would save its independence.

? [01] What did the Sivas Congress (4–11 September 1919) achieve that Erzurum had not yet done?
- Ended the war with France ~~ That happened with the 1921 Ankara Agreement.
* Unified all regional resistance associations into one national organization ~~ It created the *Anadolu ve Rumeli Müdafaa-i Hukuk Cemiyeti* and rejected all mandates.
- Signed the Treaty of Gümrü ~~ Signed in December 1920 on the Eastern Front.
- Proclaimed the Republic ~~ Proclaimed on 29 October 1923.
! While Erzurum was a regional eastern congress, Sivas unified resistance groups nationwide.

? [01] What was the *Misak-ı Millî* (National Pact) adopted on 28 January 1920?
- A peace treaty signed with Britain ~~ It was a parliamentary declaration, not a treaty with Britain.
* A declaration by the last Ottoman Parliament defining Turkey's minimum borders and full independence ~~ It set the political goals of the National Struggle.
- A military alliance with Italy ~~ No such alliance existed.
- A new constitution for the Sultan ~~ The first TBMM constitution (*Teşkilât-ı Esasiye*) was adopted in 1921.
! Adopted by the last Ottoman Parliament in Istanbul, *Misak-ı Millî* stated the core borders and independence demands.

? [01] What happened on 16 March 1920 that directly led to opening a new parliament in Ankara?
- The Battle of Sakarya ~~ Fought in 1921.
* Allied forces officially occupied Istanbul and dissolved the Ottoman Parliament ~~ Deputies fled to Ankara to form the TBMM.
- The Treaty of Lausanne was signed ~~ Signed in 1923.
- The Great Offensive began ~~ Began on 26 August 1922.
! After the official occupation of Istanbul on 16 March 1920, Mustafa Kemal called for a new assembly in Ankara.

? [01] When did the Grand National Assembly (TBMM) open in Ankara?
- 19 May 1919 ~~ Landing at Samsun.
- 28 January 1920 ~~ Adoption of *Misak-ı Millî*.
* 23 April 1920 ~~ The TBMM opened in Ankara on 23 April 1920 with Mustafa Kemal as speaker.
- 10 August 1920 ~~ Date of the Treaty of Sèvres.
! 23 April 1920 marked the opening of the TBMM (National Sovereignty and Children's Day).

? [01] Why did the Treaty of Sèvres (10 August 1920) never take legal effect?
- Britain refused to sign it ~~ Britain was one of the signatories.
* The Ottoman Parliament never ratified it, and the TBMM government in Ankara rejected it ~~ It was legally dead and overturned by military victory.
- It only applied to Eastern Thrace ~~ It aimed to partition Anatolia itself.
- It was replaced immediately in 1920 ~~ It was replaced by the Treaty of Lausanne in 1923.
! Under the Ottoman constitution, treaties required parliamentary ratification; Ankara rejected Sèvres completely.

? [01] Which treaty in December 1920 marked the TBMM's first major diplomatic and military success on the Eastern Front?

? [01] After which 22-day battle in August–September 1921 did the TBMM grant Mustafa Kemal the rank of Marshal (*Mareşal*) and the title *Gazi*?

? [01] What did the Ankara Agreement of 20 October 1921 achieve?
- Peace with Greece ~~ Peace with Greece came after Mudanya (1922) and Lausanne (1923).

? [01] On what date did the Great Offensive (*Büyük Taarruz*) begin, leading to the decisive victory at Dumlupınar on 30 August?

- 11 October 1922 ~~ The Armistice of Mudanya was signed on 11 October 1922.

? [01] What did the Armistice of Mudanya (11 October 1922) secure without further fighting?

- Immediate withdrawal from Mosul ~~ Mosul remained unresolved at Lausanne and was settled in 1926.
! Mudanya ended the war on the Western Front and handed Eastern Thrace and Istanbul back to Turkish control.

? [01] When was the Treaty of Lausanne (*Lozan Barış Antlaşması*) signed?
