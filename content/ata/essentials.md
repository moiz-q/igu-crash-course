One story in four steps — from a weakening empire to an independent Turkey.

:::idea The four phases
@widget viz name=ata-phases

:::idea Key turning points
- **Mudros (30 Oct 1918)** — defeat; the Allies may occupy the country.
- **Samsun (19 May 1919)** — Mustafa Kemal starts the National Struggle.
- **TBMM opens (23 Apr 1920)** — a new parliament in Ankara.
- **Sèvres (1920)** would have divided Anatolia → rejected; **Lausanne (24 Jul 1923)** recognises full independence.

## Quick check

? Which event is seen as the start of the National Struggle?

* Mustafa Kemal landing at Samsun on 19 May 1919 ~~ correct
- the Tanzimat Edict ~~ that was 1839
- the Treaty of Lausanne ~~ that ended it
! 19 May 1919 — Samsun.

? Which agreement let the Allies occupy Ottoman lands after WWI?
* the Armistice of Mudros (1918) ~~ correct
- the Armistice of Mudanya (1922) ~~ that ended the fighting in Türkiye's favour
- the Ankara Agreement (1921) ~~ that was with France
- the Treaty of Lausanne (1923) ~~ that recognised independence
! Mudros = defeat; Mudanya = end of fighting.

? When did the Grand National Assembly (TBMM) open?
- 29 October 1923 ~~ that's the Republic
- 19 May 1919 ~~ that's Samsun
* 23 April 1920 ~~ correct
- 30 August 1922 ~~ that's the Battle of Dumlupınar
! 23 April 1920 in Ankara.

? Which treaty recognised Turkey's full independence?
- Sèvres ~~ it tried to divide Anatolia and was never applied
* Lausanne ~~ 24 July 1923
- Mudros ~~ an armistice of defeat
- Gümrü ~~ a treaty on the Eastern Front
! Lausanne replaced Sèvres.
