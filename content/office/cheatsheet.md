title: Office Applications — Cheat Sheet

## 1. Search, Email & Cloud

- Search: `"exact phrase"` · `site:edu.tr` · `filetype:pdf` · `-exclude`.
- Email: **To** (action), **CC** (visible info), **BCC** (hidden list—use for bulk emails!).
- Cloud sharing: **Viewer** (read-only) vs. **Commenter** vs. **Editor**.

## 2. Microsoft Word

- **Table of Contents**: Apply `Home → Styles → Heading 1/2`, then `References → Table of Contents`.
- **Page Break** (`Ctrl+Enter`) vs. **Section Break** (`Layout → Breaks → Next Page`, needed for mixed Portrait/Landscape or separate headers).
- **Hide cover page number**: Header & Footer → **Different First Page**.
- **Captions & Citations**: `References → Insert Caption` · `References → Insert Citation / Bibliography`.
- **Review & Export**: `Review → Track Changes` (`Ctrl+Shift+E`) · `File → Export → Create PDF/XPS`.

## 3. Microsoft Excel

- Always start formulas with **`=`**. Lock fixed cells with **`$A$1`** (`F4`).
- Functions: `SUM` (`TOPLA`), `AVERAGE` (`ORTALAMA`), `MIN`, `MAX`, `COUNT` (numbers), `COUNTA` (non-empty), `IF` (`EĞER`), `COUNTIF`, `ROUND`.
- Sort/Filter: click 1 cell inside table → `Data → Filter` (`Ctrl+Shift+L`).
- Charts: **Column/Bar** (compare categories), **Line** (time trend), **Pie** (% parts of whole).
- Freeze header: `View → Freeze Panes → Freeze Top Row`.

## 4. Microsoft PowerPoint

- **Slide Master** (`View → Slide Master`): put global logos/fonts/footers here, then **Close Master View**.
- **Design**: 16:9 widescreen, **6×6 rule**, ≥ 20 pt body font, high contrast, resize images from **corner** handles.
- **Showtime**: `F5` (from start), `Shift+F5` (current slide), `B` (black screen), **Notes** pane + **Presenter View**, export `.pdf` backup.
