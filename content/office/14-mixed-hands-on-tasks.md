## Part 2: Complete mini-project workflow

1. **Excel**: Build a 5-student grade sheet (`Name`, `Midterm`, `Final`, `Weighted Avg = 0.4*Mid + 0.6*Fin`, `Status = IF(Avg>=60, "Pass", "Fail")`), add `AVERAGE`/`MAX`/`MIN`, and insert a **Column Chart**.
2. **Word**: Write a 2-page summary report using **Heading 1 / Heading 2** styles, an automatic **Table of Contents**, a pasted table/chart with **Insert Caption**, and export to **`.pdf`**.
3. **PowerPoint**: Build a 4-slide **16:9** summary deck with a **Slide Master** footer, **6×6** bullets, the Excel chart, and export a **`.pdf`** backup.
