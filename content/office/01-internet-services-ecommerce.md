University courses expect you to search academic sources, email instructors professionally, and recognize common online business models.

## Smarter web searching

| Operator | What it does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `"exact phrase"` | Matches words in that exact order | `"Istanbul Gelisim University" syllabus` |
| `site:domain` | Searches only inside one website/domain | `site:edu.tr "C programming" notes` |
| `filetype:pdf` | Finds downloadable documents (PDF, DOCX, PPTX) | `"database normalization" filetype:pdf` |
| `-word` | Excludes unwanted results | `python tutorial -snake` |

## Professional email etiquette (To, CC, BCC)

- **To**: the main person(s) who need to read and reply.
- **CC (Carbon Copy)**: people who should stay informed (everyone sees their addresses).
- **BCC (Blind Carbon Copy)**: hidden recipients—use **BCC** when emailing a large group of students or clients so you do not leak everyone's email address!
- **Subject line**: short and specific (e.g., `CMP151 Sec 2 — Question about Lab 3`). Never leave it blank, and never write an entire paragraph in the subject.

## E-commerce models

- **B2C (Business-to-Consumer)**: online store selling directly to individual shoppers (e.g., Trendyol retail, Amazon).
- **B2B (Business-to-Business)**: one company selling wholesale goods or software to another company (e.g., cloud hosting or bulk hardware).
- **C2C (Consumer-to-Consumer)**: individuals selling to other individuals via a platform (e.g., Sahibinden, Dolap).
- **C2B (Consumer-to-Business)**: individuals offering services/content to companies (e.g., freelance photographers selling stock photos or influencers).

## How the web works in one minute

- **Internet vs. Web.** The **Internet** is the worldwide network of connected computers (the roads). The **World Wide Web** is one service that runs on it: pages linked together and opened in a browser (the shops on the roads). Email, online games and video calls also use the Internet but are not "the Web".
- **URL** (address): `https://www.gelisim.edu.tr/tr/page` = **protocol** (`https`) + **domain name** (`www.gelisim.edu.tr`) + **path** (`/tr/page`).
- **HTTP vs. HTTPS.** HTTP sends pages as plain text. **HTTPS** encrypts them (the **padlock** icon). Never type a password on a page without HTTPS.
- **DNS** turns a name like `gelisim.edu.tr` into the server's IP address — like a phone book.

## Browser settings you should know

| Feature | What it is | Where (Chrome / Edge) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cookies** | small files a website saves in your browser to remember you (login, cart, language) | Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies / Third-party cookies |
| **Cache** | saved copies of page files so sites load faster | Settings → Privacy → **Clear browsing data** (`Ctrl+Shift+Delete`) |
| **History** | list of pages you visited | `Ctrl+H` |
| **Private / Incognito window** | does **not** save history, cookies or form data after you close it (your school or ISP can still see traffic) | `Ctrl+Shift+N` (Chrome) / `Ctrl+Shift+P` (Edge, Firefox) |
| **Bookmarks / Favourites** | saved links | `Ctrl+D` |
| **Home page / startup** | page that opens first | Settings → On startup |

## Email threats

- **Phishing:** a fake email that pretends to be from your bank, university or a delivery company and asks you to "verify your password" on a fake site. Check the **sender's real address** and **hover over links** before clicking.
- **Spam:** unwanted mass email (adverts, scams). Mark it as spam; don't reply.
- **Malicious attachments:** files such as `.exe`, `.scr`, `.js` or Office files with **macros** that install malware when opened. Don't open attachments you were not expecting.

**Email fields recap:** **To** = main receivers · **CC** (carbon copy) = for information; everyone sees them · **BCC** (blind carbon copy) = hidden receivers; others cannot see these addresses (use it for mass emails to protect privacy).

## All five e-commerce models

| Model | Who sells → who buys | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **B2B** | business → business | a factory buys parts from a supplier |

## Common mistakes

- Putting 40 classmates in **CC** instead of **BCC**—that exposes everyone's private email address.
- Writing *"urgent"* or *"help"* as an email subject without a course code or topic.
- Confusing **B2B** (business selling to business) with **C2C** (person selling to person).

## Key terms

- BCC (Blind Carbon Copy) | email field that hides recipients' addresses from each other | gizli kopya (BCC)
- search operator | special keyword (`site:`, `filetype:`, `""`) that narrows web search results | arama operatörü
- B2C | Business-to-Consumer e-commerce | işletmeden tüketiciye (B2C)
- B2B | Business-to-Business e-commerce | işletmeden işletmeye (B2B)

## Quick check

? You want to email 35 classmates a group invitation without showing everyone's email address to the whole list. Which field should you put their addresses in?
- To ~~ everyone in `To` is visible to all recipients
- CC ~~ everyone in `CC` is visible to all recipients
* BCC ~~ Blind Carbon Copy hides recipients from each other
- Subject ~~ Subject is the email title, not an address field
! Always use BCC for bulk emails to protect recipients' privacy.

? Which search query finds only **PDF** files about SQL on `.edu.tr` university sites?
- `SQL pdf edu.tr` ~~ returns normal web pages mentioning those words
* `SQL filetype:pdf site:edu.tr` ~~ restricts both file format (`pdf`) and domain (`edu.tr`)
- `"SQL" -pdf` ~~ excludes the word `pdf`
- `site:pdf filetype:edu.tr` ~~ `site:` and `filetype:` are swapped
! Combine `filetype:pdf` and `site:edu.tr` to target university PDF handouts.

? A student sells a used textbook to another student on an online marketplace. Which e-commerce model is this?
- B2B ~~ business to business
- B2C ~~ business to consumer
* C2C ~~ Consumer-to-Consumer (two individuals trading via a platform)
- C2B ~~ consumer selling to a business
! Individuals selling to other individuals is C2C.
