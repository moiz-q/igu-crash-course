Slides are **visual aids** for your audience, not a Word document projected onto a wall. If the audience is reading paragraphs on your slide, they stop listening to you!

## Golden rules of slide design

- **The 6×6 rule**: aim for at most **6 bullets per slide** and **6 words per bullet** (keywords, not full sentences).
- **Readable font sizes**: Titles **32–44 pt**, body text **20–28 pt** (never below 18 pt!). Use clean sans-serif fonts (Calibri, Arial, Segoe UI).
- **High contrast**: dark text on a light background (safest in bright classrooms) or crisp white text on a deep dark background. Never put yellow text on white or dark blue text on black!
- **One idea per slide**: split dense topics across two slides rather than shrinking text to 12 pt.

## SmartArt, Tables & Charts

- **SmartArt** (`Insert → Illustrations → SmartArt`): turns plain bullet lists into **Process** arrows, **Hierarchy** org charts, or **Cycle** diagrams. (You can also right-click an existing bullet list → **Convert to SmartArt**!)
- **Tables & Charts**: `Insert → Table` or `Insert → Chart`. If you copy a chart from Excel, **Paste → Keep Source Formatting & Link Data** updates the slide chart automatically if the Excel numbers change!

## Designer (Design Ideas)

**Design → Designer** (called *Design Ideas* in older versions) suggests professional layouts for the current slide when you insert a picture, a list or a chart. Click a thumbnail to apply it. It needs an internet connection and a Microsoft 365 subscription.

## Common mistakes

- Pasting whole paragraphs from Wikipedia onto a slide in 12 pt font and reading them aloud word for word.
- Using low-contrast backgrounds (busy photos behind text without a dark/light overlay box).
- Stretching images by dragging only the **side** handle instead of a **corner** handle (distorts faces and logos!).

## Key terms

- 6×6 rule | guideline of at most 6 bullets per slide and 6 words per bullet | 6×6 kuralı
- SmartArt | built-in visual diagrams (Process, Hierarchy, Cycle) for structured ideas | SmartArt grafiği
- contrast | visual difference between text color and background color for readability | kontrast

* At most ~6 bullet points per slide and ~6 words per bullet ~~ keeps slides scannable instead of walls of text
- 6 colors and 6 fonts per slide ~~ use at most 2 fonts and a small palette
- 6-minute presentation at 6 pm ~~ unrelated
! The 6×6 rule prevents text-heavy, unreadable slides.

? How should you resize a photo or logo on a slide so it does **not** look stretched or squashed?
- Drag the top edge handle ~~ distorts height only
- Drag the right edge handle ~~ distorts width only
* Drag a **corner** handle (or hold `Shift` if needed) ~~ corner handles preserve aspect ratio
- Change the slide size ~~ changes the whole deck
! Always resize images from a **corner** handle to keep proportions intact.

? Which PowerPoint feature converts a plain bulleted process list into a visual diagram in one click?
- `Design → Variants` ~~ changes theme colors
* `Home → Paragraph → Convert to SmartArt` ~~ turns bullets directly into SmartArt diagrams
- `Transitions → Morph` ~~ slide-to-slide transition
- `Review → Compare` ~~ compares two decks
! Convert to SmartArt turns text lists into diagrams immediately.
