# Office Applications MCQ Bank (Part A: Lessons 01–06)

? [01] Which Google search operator restricts results to a single domain such as `.edu.tr`?
- `filetype:` ~~ filters by file extension (e.g., `pdf`)
* `site:` ~~ `site:edu.tr` searches only within `.edu.tr` domains
- `inurl:` ~~ matches URL text, not strictly the domain
- `-` ~~ excludes a keyword
! Use `site:edu.tr` to limit search results to Turkish university domains.

? [01] Which search query finds the exact phrase `computer networks` in PDF format while excluding the word `cisco`?
- `computer networks pdf -cisco` ~~ does not require exact phrase or PDF filetype
* `"computer networks" filetype:pdf -cisco` ~~ quotes force exact phrase, `filetype:pdf` filters format, `-cisco` excludes
- `site:pdf "computer networks"` ~~ `site:` expects a domain, not a file type
- `"computer networks" +pdf` ~~ `+` does not filter by file extension
! Combine `"..."`, `filetype:pdf`, and `-word` for targeted academic searches.

? [01] When sending an announcement to 50 students who do not know each other, where should you put their email addresses?
- `To` ~~ exposes everyone's email address
- `CC` ~~ exposes everyone's email address
* `BCC` ~~ hides recipients' addresses from one another
- `Reply-To` ~~ sets where replies go
! Always use BCC for group announcements to protect privacy.

? [01] What does **CC** stand for in email?

* Carbon Copy ~~ visible copy sent to people who should stay informed
- Closed Copy ~~ incorrect
- Cloud Copy ~~ incorrect
! CC = Carbon Copy (recipients are visible to all).

? [01] A company sells cloud accounting software to other businesses. Which e-commerce model is this?
- B2C ~~ business to individual consumer
* B2B ~~ Business-to-Business
- C2C ~~ consumer to consumer
- C2B ~~ consumer to business
! Business selling products or services to another business is B2B.

? [01] An individual sells a second-hand laptop to another individual on an online marketplace. Which model is this?
- B2B ~~ business to business
- B2C ~~ business to consumer
* C2C ~~ Consumer-to-Consumer
- G2C ~~ government to citizen
! Person-to-person sales via a platform are C2C.

? [01] A freelance designer sells custom icons to a software company. Which e-commerce model best describes this?
- B2B ~~ two companies
- B2C ~~ company selling to consumer
- C2C ~~ two consumers
* C2B ~~ Consumer-to-Business (individual providing value to a business)
! An individual offering goods or services to a business is C2B.

? [01] Which email subject line is **most professional** when asking a professor about a lab grade?

* `CMP151 Sec 1 — Question on Lab 4 Grade` ~~ clear course, section, and topic
- `Can you check my grade please because I think there is a mistake on question 3` ~~ way too long for a subject line
! A good subject line is concise and includes the course code and topic.

? [01] What does the minus sign (`-`) do in a Google search like `java -coffee`?
- Searches only for `coffee` ~~ opposite
* Excludes results containing the word `coffee` ~~ keeps results focused on Java programming
- Searches for exact phrase `"java coffee"` ~~ quotes do exact phrase
- Forces PDF results ~~ `filetype:pdf` does that
! Prefixing a word with `-` (no space) removes pages containing that word.

? [02] You need to submit 5 separate `.c` source files through a portal that only allows **1 file** upload. What should you do?

? [02] What is a `.csv` file?

- An encrypted PDF ~~ `.pdf`
- A Word macro template ~~ `.dotm`
! CSV stands for Comma-Separated Values.

? [02] Why is it good practice to enable **File name extensions** in Windows File Explorer?
