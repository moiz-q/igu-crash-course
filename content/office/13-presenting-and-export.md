Great slides still fail if fonts break on the classroom PC or you run out of time. Here is how to finish and deliver cleanly.

## Rehearsing & Slide Show shortcuts

- **Rehearse Timings** (`Slide Show → Set Up → Rehearse Timings`): practices your talk with a live timer per slide.
- **Live Slide Show shortcuts**:
  - `N` / `Space` / `→` : Next slide or animation
  - `P` / `←` : Previous
  - `B` : Black screen (pauses audience attention on you!) · `W` : White screen
  - `Ctrl+P` : Pen tool to draw/highlight live on a slide (`E` erases)
  - `Esc` : End Slide Show

## Exporting & sharing safely

- **Export as PDF** (`File → Export → Create PDF/XPS`): **never** shifts fonts or layouts, even on Linux/Mac classroom PCs! Always keep a PDF backup on your USB/cloud.
- **Print Handouts** (`File → Print → Full Page Slides` → change to `3 Slides` or `6 Slides Horizontal`): prints multiple slides per page (with note lines on `3 Slides`).
- **Export as Video** (`File → Export → Create a Video`): turns a timed/narrated deck into an `.mp4`.
- **Embed fonts** (`File → Options → Save → Embed fonts in the file`): protects custom `.pptx` fonts when opening on another Windows PC.

## Common mistakes

- Bringing only a `.pptx` using a downloaded custom font—on the lab PC, PowerPoint substitutes Arial and overflows text boxes! Always **embed fonts** or bring a **PDF** backup.
- Pressing `Esc` instead of `B` when you want the audience to look at you during a discussion—`Esc` exposes your messy PowerPoint editing window.
- Reading slides with your back turned to the audience instead of facing them and glancing at Presenter View.

## Quick check

? During a live PowerPoint Slide Show, which key turns the projector screen **black** so the audience focuses on you?
- `Esc` ~~ exits Slide Show into the editor
* `B` ~~ toggles a solid black screen (`B` again resumes)
- `F5` ~~ restarts Slide Show
- `Ctrl+S` ~~ saves
! Press `B` for black screen (or `W` for white screen) during a live talk.

? You designed a `.pptx` at home with a custom font and will present on an unfamiliar university lab PC. What is the safest way to guarantee your layout never breaks?
- Delete all titles ~~ unhelpful
* Export a **PDF** backup (`File → Export → Create PDF/XPS`) and/or enable **Embed fonts** in `.pptx` ~~ PDF locks exact layout on any computer
- Save as `.txt` ~~ loses slides and images
- Turn off Slide Master ~~ doesn't protect fonts
! Always bring a PDF backup (and embed fonts in `.pptx`).

? Which Print layout prints **3 slides per page** with blank lines beside each slide for audience notes?
- Full Page Slides ~~ 1 slide per page, no note lines
* `3 Slides` (under Handouts) ~~ standard PowerPoint note-taking handout layout
- Outline ~~ text only, no slide thumbnails
- Notes Pages ~~ 1 slide + presenter notes per page
! The `3 Slides` handout layout includes writing lines next to each thumbnail.
