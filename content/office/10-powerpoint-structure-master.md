A clean presentation looks unified from Slide 1 to Slide 15. Instead of pasting a logo and changing fonts manually on every slide, PowerPoint gives you **Slide Master**.

## Themes, Layouts, and Slide Size

- **Slide Size**: `Design → Customize → Slide Size → Widescreen (16:9)` (modern standard for projectors and screens).
- **Theme**: `Design → Themes` sets coordinated colors, fonts, and backgrounds across the whole deck.
- **Slide Layout**: `Home → Slides → Layout` lets you pick **Title Slide**, **Title and Content**, **Two Content**, **Comparison**, or **Blank**. Always use built-in placeholders rather than drawing raw text boxes everywhere!

## Slide Master (`View → Master Views → Slide Master`)

**Slide Master** is the blueprint behind every slide:
- Edit the **top-most Master slide** (the big slide at #1) to change the font, footer, or add a university logo to **every** slide at once!
- Edit a **child layout** below it to customize only one layout (e.g., Title Slide).
- Click **Slide Master → Close Master View** when finished so you don't accidentally write your presentation content inside the Master!

## Headers, footers & slide numbers

`Insert → Text → Header & Footer` → tick **Slide number** and **Footer** (e.g., course name) → tick **Don't show on title slide** → **Apply to All**.

## Try it

:::checklist Lesson 10 hands-on checklist
- Confirmed slide size is **16:9 Widescreen** (`Design → Slide Size`)
- Opened `View → Slide Master`, placed a small logo or accent bar on the top Master slide, then clicked **Close Master View**
- Added slide numbers via `Insert → Header & Footer` with **Don't show on title slide** checked
- Created 3 slides using built-in **Layouts** (`Title Slide`, `Title and Content`, `Two Content`)
:::

## Common mistakes

- Typing actual presentation text while still inside **Slide Master** view—every new slide then inherits that text! Always click **Close Master View** first.
- Pasting a university logo onto 20 slides one by one instead of placing it once on the **Slide Master**.
- Using `4:3` Standard on a modern `16:9` classroom projector (leaves black bars on both sides).

## Key terms

- Slide Master | the template blueprint (`View → Slide Master`) controlling fonts, logos, and layouts across all slides | asıl slayt
- Slide Layout | a predefined arrangement of title, text, and media placeholders (`Home → Layout`) | slayt düzeni
- Theme | a coordinated set of colors, fonts, and effects (`Design → Themes`) | tema

## Quick check

? You want your university logo to appear in the exact same top-right corner on **every** slide without copying it 20 times. Where should you place it?
- On Slide 1 only ~~ it will only appear on Slide 1
* On the top-level slide in `View → Slide Master` ~~ changes on the top Master slide apply across all layouts
- Inside Notes Page view ~~ Notes view is for speaker notes
- Inside Slide Sorter view ~~ Slide Sorter reorders slides
! Place global logos, fonts, and footers on the top Slide Master.

? Which menu path adds slide numbers while hiding the number on Slide 1 (the title slide)?
- `Design → Format Background` ~~ changes slide background
* `Insert → Header & Footer` → check **Slide number** and **Don't show on title slide** → **Apply to All** ~~ standard path
- `View → Reading View` ~~ preview mode
- `Animations → Add Animation` ~~ animations are for object motion
! `Insert → Header & Footer` controls slide numbers, footers, and title-slide suppression.

? What is the most common mistake students make after editing `View → Slide Master`?
- Saving as `.pptx` ~~ `.pptx` is the normal format
* Forgetting to click **Close Master View** and typing slide content directly into the master layouts ~~ puts their text onto the template itself
- Using 16:9 widescreen ~~ 16:9 is recommended
- Applying a Theme ~~ Themes are designed to be applied
! Always click **Close Master View** before authoring individual slides.
