## Quick check

? Which plural is correct?
- okul-ler ~~ okul has back vowels → -lar
* okul-lar ~~ back vowel u → back suffix
- ev-lar ~~ ev has a front vowel → -ler
- kitap-ler ~~ kitap's last vowel is a → -lar
! Great vowel harmony.

? In a normal Turkish sentence, where does the verb usually go?
- first ~~ that's not the usual order
- second ~~ that's English
* last ~~ Subject–Object–Verb
- anywhere, it never matters ~~ word order is usually SOV
! Turkish is SOV.

? A language that builds words by adding many suffixes to a root is called:
* agglutinative ~~ correct (sondan eklemeli)
- isolating ~~ like Chinese, few suffixes
- tonal ~~ meaning depends on pitch
- inflectional ~~ like Arabic, roots change inside
! Turkish = agglutinative (eklemeli).
