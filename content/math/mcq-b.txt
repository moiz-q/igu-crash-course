? [08] If $x - y = 5$ and $xy = 6$, what is $x^2 + y^2$?
- 13 ~~ $25 - 12 = 13$, wait: $(x-y)^2 = x^2 - 2xy + y^2 \implies 25 = x^2+y^2 - 12 \implies x^2+y^2 = 37$!
- 25 ~~ forgot $+2xy$
- 31 ~~ added $xy$ instead of $2xy$
* 37 ~~ $x^2 + y^2 = (x - y)^2 + 2xy = 25 + 12 = 37$
- 49 ~~ $(x+y)^2$
! $(x-y)^2 = x^2 - 2xy + y^2 \implies x^2 + y^2 = 5^2 + 2(6) = 37$.

? [08] Factor completely: $3x^2 - 27$:
- $3(x - 9)$ ~~ $x^2$, not $x$
- $(3x - 9)(x + 3)$ ~~ factors out another 3
* $3(x - 3)(x + 3)$ ~~ factor 3 first: $3(x^2 - 9) = 3(x - 3)(x + 3)$
- $3(x - 3)^2$ ~~ $(x-3)^2 = x^2 - 6x + 9$
- $(x - 3)(x + 3)$ ~~ lost the factor of 3

? [08] Simplify $\frac{x^3 - 8}{x^2 + 2x + 4}$:
- $x + 2$ ~~ $x^3 - 8 = (x - 2)(x^2 + 2x + 4)$
* $x - 2$ ~~ numerator factors as $(x - 2)(x^2 + 2x + 4)$, canceling the denominator
- $x - 4$ ~~ $8 = 2^3$, not $4^3$

- $1$ ~~ not equal
! Difference of cubes: $x^3 - 2^3 = (x - 2)(x^2 + 2x + 4)$.

? [08] If $x + \frac{1}{x} = 4$, what is $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}$?

- 12 ~~ subtracted 4
- 8 ~~ $4 \times 2$

? [08] Factor $2x^2 + 5x - 3$:
- $(2x + 1)(x - 3)$ ~~ gives $-5x$ in the middle
* $(2x - 1)(x + 3)$ ~~ $2x\cdot 3 - 1\cdot x = +5x$ and $(-1)(3) = -3$
- $(2x + 3)(x - 1)$ ~~ gives $+x$ in the middle
- $(2x - 3)(x + 1)$ ~~ gives $-x$ in the middle

? [09] Solve $\frac{x + 2}{3} - \frac{x - 1}{2} = 1$:

- $x = 5$ ~~ check: $7/3 - 2 = 1/3 \ne 1$

? [09] Solve the system: $2x + 3y = 13$ and $x - y = 4$:

- $(6, 2)$ ~~ $2(6) + 6 = 18 \ne 13$

? [09] For which $a$ does $(a - 3)x + 2 = 2x + b$ hold for **all** real $x$ (infinitely many solutions) when $b = 2$?

? [09] The sum of three consecutive integers is `57`. What is the **largest** of the three?

? [10] What is the **discriminant** ($\Delta$) of $2x^2 - 4x + 5 = 0$?
- $-24$ ~~ $\Delta = (-4)^2 - 4(2)(5) = 16 - 40 = -24$! Wait—let's mark $-24$ as `*`!
* $-24$ ~~ $\Delta = (-4)^2 - 4(2)(5) = 16 - 40 = -24$ (no real roots)

- $56$ ~~ $16 + 40$ (sign error on $-4ac$)
- $0$ ~~ $16 - 40 \ne 0$

? [10] If $x_1$ and $x_2$ are the roots of $3x^2 + 9x - 12 = 0$, what is $x_1 + x_2$?
- $3$ ~~ forgot minus sign in $-b/a$

- $-4$ ~~ $-4$ is $c/a = x_1 x_2$
- $4$ ~~ $+4$

! By Vieta's formula, $x_1 + x_2 = -b/a = -9/3 = -3$.

? [10] If $x_1, x_2$ are the roots of $x^2 - 6x + 4 = 0$, find $\frac{1}{x_1} + \frac{1}{x_2}$:

- $4$ ~~ that is $x_1 x_2$

? [10] Which quadratic equation has roots $x_1 = 2$ and $x_2 = -5$?
- $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$ ~~ sum is $+3$, should be $-3$ ($x^2 + 3x - 10 = 0$)

? [10] For what positive value of $k$ does $x^2 + kx + 16 = 0$ have **one repeated real root**?

? [13] 40 grams of salt are dissolved in 160 grams of water. What is the **salt percentage** of the mixture?
- 25% ~~ divided $40 / 160$ instead of $40 / (40 + 160)$
* 20% ~~ total weight $= 40 + 160 = 200$ g; $40 / 200 = 20\%$
- 16% ~~ incorrect
- 40% ~~ 40 g out of 200 g, not 100 g

! Salt percentage $= \frac{\text{salt}}{\text{total mixture}} = \frac{40}{40 + 160} = \frac{40}{200} = 20\%$.
