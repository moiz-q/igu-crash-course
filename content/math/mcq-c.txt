# Mathematics I MCQ Bank (Part C: Extra 5-option practice)

? [01] If $s(A) = 8$ and $s(B) = 6$, and $A \cap B = \emptyset$, what is $s(A \cup B)$?
- 2 ~~ subtracted instead of adding
- 10 ~~ no overlap to subtract
* 14 ~~ disjoint sets have $s(A \cap B) = 0$, so $8 + 6 = 14$
- 48 ~~ multiplied
- 7 ~~ incorrect
! Disjoint sets ($A \cap B = \emptyset$) satisfy $s(A \cup B) = s(A) + s(B) = 14$.

? [02] Simplify $\frac{2}{5} + \frac{3}{10} - \frac{1}{2}$:
- $\frac{1}{10}$ ~~ $\frac{4 + 3 - 5}{10} = \frac{2}{10} = \frac{1}{5}$
* $\frac{1}{5}$ ~~ $\frac{4}{10} + \frac{3}{10} - \frac{5}{10} = \frac{2}{10} = \frac{1}{5}$
- $\frac{3}{5}$ ~~ added $\frac{1}{2}$ instead of subtracting
- $\frac{2}{5}$ ~~ arithmetic slip
- $0$ ~~ $4+3-5 = 2 \ne 0$
! Common denominator 10 gives $\frac{4 + 3 - 5}{10} = \frac{2}{10} = \frac{1}{5}$.

? [03] What is the remainder when `123456` is divided by **3**?
* 0 ~~ $1+2+3+4+5+6 = 21$, which is divisible by 3
- 1 ~~ sum is 21
- 2 ~~ sum is 21
- 3 ~~ remainder mod 3 must be $< 3$
- 6 ~~ remainder mod 3 must be $< 3$
! Digit sum is $21 = 3 \times 7$, so remainder is $0$.

? [03] Find the largest possible value of $a + b$ if the 4-digit number `2a4b` is divisible by **15**:
- 9 ~~ $b=0, a=9 \implies 9$
- 10 ~~ larger exists
* 12 ~~ for $b=5$, $2+a+4+5 = 11+a$ is div by 3 when $a=7$, giving $7+5=12$
- 14 ~~ $a=9, b=5$ gives sum $20$ (not div by 3)
- 15 ~~ impossible
! Checking $b=5$ gives max $a=7$ ($a+b=12$); checking $b=0$ gives max $a=9$ ($a+b=9$). Largest $a+b$ is $12$.

? [04] Convert $(11101)_2$ to decimal:

* 29 ~~ $16 + 8 + 4 + 0 + 1 = 29$
- 30 ~~ $16+8+4+2$

? [04] What is $123 \bmod 7$?
- 2 ~~ $7 \times 17 = 119$
- 3 ~~ $123 - 119 = 4$
* 4 ~~ $123 = 7 \times 17 + 4$
- 5 ~~ $124 \bmod 7 = 5$
- 6 ~~ $125 \bmod 7 = 6$
! $7 \times 17 = 119$, and $123 - 119 = 4$.

? [05] What is $\gcd(45, 75)$?
- 5 ~~ 15 is larger
- 9 ~~ 9 does not divide 75
* 15 ~~ $45 = 3^2 \cdot 5$ and $75 = 3 \cdot 5^2 \implies \gcd = 3 \cdot 5 = 15$
- 25 ~~ 25 does not divide 45
- 225 ~~ 225 is the LCM, not GCD
! $\gcd(45, 75) = 15$.

? [06] Evaluate $4^{-1/2}$:
- $-2$ ~~ negative exponent means reciprocal, not negative sign
- $-\frac{1}{2}$ ~~ positive base stays positive
* $\frac{1}{2}$ ~~ $4^{-1/2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4}} = \frac{1}{2}$
- $2$ ~~ forgot the minus sign in the exponent
- $\frac{1}{16}$ ~~ $4^{-2} = 1/16$
! $4^{-1/2} = \frac{1}{4^{1/2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4}} = \frac{1}{2}$.

? [07] Simplify $\sqrt{18} \cdot \sqrt{8}$:
- $6$ ~~ $\sqrt{144} = 12$
- $8$ ~~ too small
* $12$ ~~ $\sqrt{18 \cdot 8} = \sqrt{144} = 12$
- $16$ ~~ $\sqrt{256}$
- $24$ ~~ doubled
! $\sqrt{18} \cdot \sqrt{8} = \sqrt{144} = 12$.

? [08] Expand $(3x - 2)^2$:
- $9x^2 - 4$ ~~ forgot the middle term $-12x$
- $9x^2 + 4$ ~~ forgot $-12x$
- $9x^2 - 6x + 4$ ~~ forgot to double $3x \cdot 2$
* $9x^2 - 12x + 4$ ~~ $(3x)^2 - 2(3x)(2) + 2^2 = 9x^2 - 12x + 4$
- $3x^2 - 12x + 4$ ~~ forgot to square $3$
! $(a-b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2 = 9x^2 - 12x + 4$.

? [08] Factor $4x^2 - 49$:
- $(2x - 7)^2$ ~~ gives $-28x$ middle term
* $(2x - 7)(2x + 7)$ ~~ difference of squares $(2x)^2 - 7^2$
- $(4x - 7)(x + 7)$ ~~ wrong coefficients
- $4(x - 7)(x + 7)$ ~~ $4 \times 49 \ne 49$
- $(2x - 49)(2x + 1)$ ~~ wrong constants
! $4x^2 - 49 = (2x)^2 - 7^2 = (2x - 7)(2x + 7)$.

? [09] Solve $5x - 3 = 2x + 12$:
- $x = 3$ ~~ $3x = 15 \implies x = 5$
- $x = 4$ ~~ $20 - 3 \ne 8 + 12$
* $x = 5$ ~~ $3x = 15 \implies x = 5$
- $x = 6$ ~~ too large
- $x = 9$ ~~ added $3$ wrong
! $5x - 2x = 12 + 3 \implies 3x = 15 \implies x = 5$.

? [09] If $x + y = 10$ and $x - y = 4$, what is $x^2 - y^2$?
- 14 ~~ added $10 + 4$
- 24 ~~ arithmetic slip
* 40 ~~ $x^2 - y^2 = (x + y)(x - y) = 10 \times 4 = 40$
- 52 ~~ $x=7, y=3 \implies 49 - 9 = 40$
- 100 ~~ $10^2$
! $(x + y)(x - y) = 10 \cdot 4 = 40$.

? [10] How many real roots does $x^2 + 4x + 5 = 0$ have?
* 0 ~~ $\Delta = 4^2 - 4(1)(5) = 16 - 20 = -4 < 0$ (no real roots)
- 1 ~~ $\Delta \ne 0$
- 2 ~~ $\Delta < 0$, not $> 0$
- 3 ~~ quadratic has at most 2 roots
- Infinitely many ~~ non-zero polynomial
! Since $\Delta = -4 < 0$, there are $0$ real roots.

? [10] For $x^2 - 8x + 12 = 0$, what is the **product** of the roots?
- $-8$ ~~ $-8$ is the negative sum
- $8$ ~~ $8$ is the sum of roots
* $12$ ~~ $x_1 x_2 = c/a = 12/1 = 12$
- $-12$ ~~ sign is $+12$
- $6$ ~~ $6$ is one root ($2$ and $6$), not the product
! By Vieta, $x_1 x_2 = c/a = 12$.

? [11] Solve $3x - 4 \le 2x + 5$:
- $x \le 1$ ~~ added 4 to both sides: $5 + 4 = 9$
* $x \le 9$ ~~ $3x - 2x \le 5 + 4 \implies x \le 9$
- $x \ge 9$ ~~ did not divide by a negative, so sign does not flip
- $x < 9$ ~~ original is $\le$, not $<$
- $x \le -1$ ~~ sign error
! Subtract $2x$ and add $4$: $x \le 9$.

? [11] Which interval represents $\{x \in \mathbb{R} : -2 \le x < 5\}$?
- $(-2, 5)$ ~~ $-2$ should be included `[`
* $[-2, 5)$ ~~ `-2` closed `[`, `5` open `)`
- $(-2, 5]$ ~~ endpoints swapped
- $[-2, 5]$ ~~ `5` should be excluded `)`
- $(-\infty, 5)$ ~~ missed lower bound $-2$
! Closed bracket `[` for $\le$ and open parenthesis `)` for $<$: $[-2, 5)$.

? [12] Solve $|3x - 6| \le 9$:
- $[-1, 5]$ ~~ $-9 \le 3x - 6 \le 9 \implies -3 \le 3x \le 15 \implies -1 \le x \le 5$!
* $[-1, 5]$ ~~ $-9 \le 3x - 6 \le 9 \implies -3 \le 3x \le 15 \implies [-1, 5]$
- $(-1, 5)$ ~~ $\le$ includes endpoints
- $[1, 5]$ ~~ $-3 / 3 = -1$, not $+1$
- $[-5, 1]$ ~~ wrong signs
- $(-\infty, -1] \cup [5, \infty)$ ~~ that is $\ge 9$
! $-9 \le 3x - 6 \le 9 \implies -3 \le 3x \le 15 \implies [-1, 5]$.

? [12] Evaluate $|-7| - |3 - 10| + |-2|$:
- $-12$ ~~ each absolute value is $\ge 0$
- $0$ ~~ $7 - 7 + 2 = 2$
* $2$ ~~ $7 - 7 + 2 = 2$
- $16$ ~~ $7 + 7 + 2$ (missed minus sign between terms)
- $-2$ ~~ $7 - 7 + 2 = +2$
! $|-7| = 7$, $|3 - 10| = |-7| = 7$, $|-2| = 2$; $7 - 7 + 2 = 2$.

? [13] A car travels at `80 km/h` and takes `3 hours` for a trip. How long will the same trip take at `60 km/h`?
- 2.25 hours ~~ used direct proportion
- 3.5 hours ~~ $60 \times 3.5 = 210 \ne 240$
* 4 hours ~~ distance $= 80 \times 3 = 240$ km; $240 / 60 = 4$ hours
- 4.5 hours ~~ $60 \times 4.5 = 270$
- 5 hours ~~ $60 \times 5 = 300$
! Speed and time are inversely proportional: $80 \times 3 = 60 \times t \implies t = 4$ hours.

? [13] A class has 18 boys and 12 girls. What **percentage** of the class are **girls**?
- 12% ~~ 12 is the count, not the percentage out of 30
- 30% ~~ 30 is total students
* 40% ~~ $12 / (18 + 12) = 12 / 30 = 0.40 = 40\%$
- 60% ~~ 60% are boys
- 66.7% ~~ $12 / 18$, not $12 / 30$
! Total students $= 18 + 12 = 30$; girls $= \frac{12}{30} = 40\%$.

? [08] If $a^2 - b^2 = 35$ and $a - b = 5$, what is $a + b$?
- 5 ~~ $35 / 5 = 7$
* 7 ~~ $(a - b)(a + b) = 35 \implies 5(a + b) = 35 \implies a + b = 7$
- 30 ~~ subtracted instead of dividing
- 40 ~~ added
- 175 ~~ multiplied
! $a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$, so $a + b = 35 / 5 = 7$.

? [09] Solve $\frac{2}{x} = \frac{6}{15}$ ($x \ne 0$):
- 3 ~~ $6 \times 3 = 18 \ne 30$
- 4 ~~ $6 \times 4 = 24 \ne 30$
* 5 ~~ $6x = 30 \implies x = 5$
- 6 ~~ $6 \times 6 = 36 \ne 30$
- 10 ~~ doubled
! Cross-multiply: $6x = 2 \cdot 15 = 30 \implies x = 5$.

? [10] Solve $x^2 - 9x = 0$:
- $x = 9$ only ~~ lost $x = 0$ by dividing by $x$!
- $x = 0$ only ~~ missed $x = 9$
* $x = 0$ or $x = 9$ ~~ factor: $x(x - 9) = 0 \implies x = 0$ or $x = 9$
- $x = 3$ or $x = -3$ ~~ that is $x^2 - 9 = 0$
- $x = -9$ or $x = 0$ ~~ $x + 9 = 0$ gives $-9$
! Never divide both sides by $x$; factor $x(x - 9) = 0$ to keep $x = 0$.

? [11] Which integer is in the solution set of $2x + 1 > 7$?
- 1 ~~ $3 > 7$ is false
- 2 ~~ $5 > 7$ is false
- 3 ~~ $7 > 7$ is false (strict `>`)
* 4 ~~ $2(4) + 1 = 9 > 7$ is true ($x > 3$)
- 0 ~~ $1 > 7$ is false
! $2x > 6 \implies x > 3$, so $4$ is the only option $> 3$.

? [13] An item's price increases from `80 TL` to `100 TL`. What is the **percentage increase**?
- 20% ~~ divided $20$ by $100$ (new) instead of $80$ (old)
* 25% ~~ $\frac{100 - 80}{80} = \frac{20}{80} = 25\%$
- 15% ~~ incorrect
- 30% ~~ incorrect
- 80% ~~ incorrect
! Percentage change $= \frac{\text{new} - \text{old}}{\text{old}} \times 100\% = \frac{20}{80} = 25\%$.
