## 1. Sets, primes, GCD & LCM

- $s(A \cup B) = s(A) + s(B) - s(A \cap B)$ · Subsets of $n$-element set: $2^n$.
- If $N = p_1^{a} p_2^{b} \cdots$, positive divisors $= (a+1)(b+1)\cdots$
- $\gcd(A, B) \times \operatorname{lcm}(A, B) = A \times B$

## 2. Divisibility & repeating decimals

- **3 / 9**: digit sum divisible by 3 / 9 · **4**: last 2 digits · **8**: last 3 digits · **11**: alternating sum (`+ - + -`).
- $0.\overline{a} = \frac{a}{9}$ · $0.a\overline{b} = \frac{ab - a}{90}$

## 3. Exponents & radicals

- $a^m a^n = a^{m+n}$ · $\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$ · $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$ · $a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$ · $a^0 = 1$ ($a \ne 0$)
- $\sqrt[n]{a^m} = a^{m/n}$ · $\sqrt{x^2} = |x|$ · Conjugate of $\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b}$ is $\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b}$.

## 4. Identities & quadratics

- $(a \pm b)^2 = a^2 \pm 2ab + b^2$ · $a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)$
- $a^3 \pm b^3 = (a \pm b)(a^2 \mp ab + b^2)$
- $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$: $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$, $x_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{\Delta}}{2a}$
- **Vieta**: $x_1 + x_2 = -\frac{b}{a}$, $x_1 x_2 = \frac{c}{a}$

## 5. Inequalities, absolute value & proportion

- Dividing by a negative **flips** $<, >, \le, \ge$. Never include denominator roots!
- $|u| < a \iff -a < u < a$ · $|u| > a \iff u < -a \text{ or } u > a$
- Direct proportion: $y/x = k$ · Inverse proportion: $x \cdot y = k$
