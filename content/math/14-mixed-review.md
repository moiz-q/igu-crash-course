## High-yield exam checklist

- **Sets & Numbers**: Inclusion–exclusion $s(A \cup B) = s(A) + s(B) - s(A \cap B)$; $2^n$ subsets.
- **Divisibility & Modular**: Split composite divisors into coprime parts (`12` → `3` and `4`); reduce bases mod $n$ first.
- **Primes, GCD, LCM**: Divisor count $= \prod (e_i + 1)$; $\gcd(A,B)\cdot\operatorname{lcm}(A,B) = A \cdot B$.
- **Exponents & Radicals**: Negative exponent flips; $\sqrt{x^2} = |x|$; rationalize with conjugates.
- **Factoring & Quadratics**: $(a\pm b)^2$, $a^2-b^2$; $\Delta = b^2-4ac$; Vieta $x_1+x_2 = -b/a$, $x_1 x_2 = c/a$.
- **Inequalities & Absolute Value**: Dividing by negative flips `<` / `>`; exclude denominator roots; $|u| = \text{neg} \implies \emptyset$.
