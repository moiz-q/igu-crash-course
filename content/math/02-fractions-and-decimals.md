Fractions appear inside almost every algebra, ratio, and equation problem. Mastering fraction arithmetic and order of operations saves easy points on midterms.

## Fraction rules (concrete examples first)

- **Simplify first**: $\frac{12}{18} = \frac{12 \div 6}{18 \div 6} = \frac{2}{3}$.
- **Add / Subtract** (common denominator):
  $$\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{8}{12} + \frac{3}{12} = \frac{11}{12}$$
- **Multiply** (top × top, bottom × bottom — cancel diagonally first!):
  $$\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{8}{9} = \frac{1}{1} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2}{3}$$
- **Divide** ("keep, change, flip"):
  $$\frac{5}{6} \div \frac{10}{3} = \frac{5}{6} \times \frac{3}{10} = \frac{1}{4}$$

## Order of operations (PEMDAS / İşlem Önceliği)

1. **Parentheses** `(...)` (innermost first)
2. **Exponents / Radicals**
3. **Multiplication & Division** (left to right)
4. **Addition & Subtraction** (left to right)

Example: $2 + 3 \times (4 - 1)^2 = 2 + 3 \times 9 = 2 + 27 = 29$ (never add $2+3$ before multiplying!).

## Repeating decimals to fractions

Shortcut formula for repeating decimals (devirli ondalık sayılar):
$$\text{Value} = \frac{\text{all digits} - \text{non-repeating digits}}{9\text{s for repeating digits, then }0\text{s for decimal non-repeating digits}}$$

- $0.\overline{7} = \frac{7}{9}$
- $1.2\overline{3} = \frac{123 - 12}{90} = \frac{111}{90} = \frac{37}{30}$

## Common mistakes

- Adding denominators when adding fractions: $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3}$ is **not** $\frac{2}{5}$; it is $\frac{3}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$.
- Canceling across addition: $\frac{2 + x}{2}$ is **not** $1 + x$! It equals $1 + \frac{x}{2}$. You can only cancel common **factors** (multiplication).
- Dividing by zero: $\frac{5}{0}$ is **undefined** (while $\frac{0}{5} = 0$).

## Key terms

- numerator | the top number of a fraction | pay
- denominator | the bottom number of a fraction (must not be zero) | payda
- reciprocal | the flipped fraction $b/a$ of $a/b$ | çarpmaya göre tersi
- repeating decimal | a decimal whose digits repeat endlessly (e.g., $0.\overline{3} = 1/3$) | devirli ondalık sayı

## Quick check

? Compute $\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{6}$:
- $\frac{2}{2}$ ~~ cannot subtract numerators and denominators directly
* $\frac{7}{12}$ ~~ common denominator 12: $\frac{9}{12} - \frac{2}{12} = \frac{7}{12}$
- $\frac{5}{12}$ ~~ $\frac{9-4}{12}$ would be wrong ($\frac{1}{6} = \frac{2}{12}$, not $\frac{4}{12}$)
- $\frac{1}{2}$ ~~ $\frac{3-1}{4}$ ignores the 6
! Common denominator is 12: $\frac{9}{12} - \frac{2}{12} = \frac{7}{12}$.

? What is $0.\overline{4} + 0.\overline{5}$?
- $0.9$ ~~ $4/9 + 5/9 = 9/9 = 1$
* $1$ ~~ $\frac{4}{9} + \frac{5}{9} = \frac{9}{9} = 1$
- $\frac{9}{10}$ ~~ repeating over 9, not 10
- $\frac{4}{5}$ ~~ incorrect conversion
! $0.\overline{4} = \frac{4}{9}$ and $0.\overline{5} = \frac{5}{9}$, summing to $\frac{9}{9} = 1$.

? Simplify $\frac{2}{3} \div \frac{4}{9}$:
- $\frac{8}{27}$ ~~ multiplied without flipping the second fraction
* $\frac{3}{2}$ ~~ $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{9}{4} = \frac{18}{12} = \frac{3}{2}$
- $\frac{2}{3}$ ~~ forgot to invert
- $\frac{6}{12}$ ~~ equals $1/2$, not $3/2$
! Keep $\frac{2}{3}$, change $\div$ to $\times$, flip $\frac{4}{9}$ to $\frac{9}{4}$ → $\frac{3}{2}$.
